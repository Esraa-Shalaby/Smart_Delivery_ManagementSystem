What the platform does

One system for the whole delivery cycle.

From the moment a shipment is created to the moment it's signed for, every step runs through the same platform.

Smart Shipment Management

Create, edit, and organize shipments with structured records that stay consistent across the whole operation.

Real-Time Tracking

Every shipment reports its live position and status, visible to customers, drivers, and managers alike.

Driver Management

Onboard drivers, monitor availability, and keep every profile and vehicle record in one place.

Delivery Assignment

Match shipments to the right driver automatically, based on location, load, and availability.

Analytics & Reports

Track delivery performance, delays, and driver output through clear, exportable reporting.

AI Logistics Assistant
